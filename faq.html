FAQs & terms

Good to know.

Visiting

Do I need to book?

We recommend it, especially for dinner and weekends, but we always keep space for walk-ins.

Can I just come for a drink?

Of course. The bar and waterside terrace are open for drinks without a booking, with snacks all day.

Do you have outdoor seating?

Yes — a south-facing terrace overlooking the Grand Union Canal. Terrace tables can be booked online. It’s part heated and part covered.

Are children welcome?

Very. There’s a children’s menu on request, and we can set up high chairs if you mention them when booking.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, inside and out. Paws on the floor.

Is Canal accessible?

Yes. The restaurant and terrace are step-free with accessible facilities. Let us know anything that would help.

Bookings

Can I change or cancel?

Yes, through the link in your confirmation email. Any trouble, email or call us.

Is there a cancellation policy?

Card details secure bookings and we ask for 24 hours’ notice. No-shows and late cancellations are charged at £40 per person for dinner and £20 per person for lunch.

Can I book for a group?

Up to 6 online, in the dining room or on the terrace. For 8 or more, private hire and events, send us an enquiry.

Do you host private events?

Yes, from big tables to exclusive hire: up to 70 seated or 120 standing.

Food & drink

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes — vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and allergy-friendly options. Mention them when booking or tell your server.

Is there a service charge?

A discretionary 13.5% service charge is added.

Corkage?

£50 per 750ml bottle of wine, £75 per 750ml of sparkling. Bringing a cake is £5 per head.

Do you take cash?

We’re cashless. All major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Gift vouchers?

Yes — digital vouchers for any amount from our gift voucher shop.

Anything else? hello@canalrestaurant.com · 020 8176 6073

The small print

Bookings

Bookings are for dining. Outdoor tables are part heated and part covered and subject to the weather. Card details secure bookings; late cancellations and no-shows are charged as set out above.

Privacy

When you book or sign up we collect your name, email, phone number and, where given, address. We don’t store card details or share your data beyond the booking and events platforms that run the restaurant. Email hello@canalrestaurant.com to see, correct or delete your data; we may need to verify your identity. Anything shared publicly on social media may stay visible there. We’ll post any changes to this policy here.

Complaints

We’d always rather hear it on the day. Otherwise email hello@canalrestaurant.com or write to Quality Restaurants Limited, MCA, One Canada Square, 9th Floor, London E14 5AA. We aim to propose a course of action within one working week.
