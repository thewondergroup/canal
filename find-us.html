Getting here

Two minutes from Westbourne Park station.

Tube

Westbourne Park (Circle, Hammersmith & City) is two minutes away. Royal Oak and Ladbroke Grove about ten.

Towpath

Walk or cycle along the canal from Little Venice or Ladbroke Grove.

Just for a drink

The bar and terrace are open all day. No booking needed.

Step-free

Restaurant and terrace are step-free with accessible facilities. Just let us know what would help.

Dogs

Welcome inside and out. Paws on the floor.

Little ones

Very welcome. Children’s menu on request and high chairs if you mention them when booking.

The terrace

South-facing, right on the water

Terrace tables book online. Part heated and part covered, so it works well beyond summer.
