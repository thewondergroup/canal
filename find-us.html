Getting here.

On foot, by tube, or by boat.

Tube

Westbourne Park (Circle, Hammersmith & City) is two minutes away. Royal Oak and Ladbroke Grove about ten.

Boat

We’re right on the Grand Union. Moor up on the towpath and walk in — tell us when you book.

Towpath

Walk or cycle along the canal from Little Venice (15 minutes) or Ladbroke Grove.

Step-free

Restaurant and terrace are step-free with accessible facilities. Just let us know what would help.

Dogs

Welcome inside and out. Paws on the floor.

Little ones

Very welcome. Children’s menu on request and high chairs if you mention them when booking.

The terrace

South-facing. Right on the water.

Terrace tables book online. Part heated and part covered, so it works well beyond summer. Drinks and snacks all day, no booking needed.
