Christmas Day at Canal

How the day goes.

Arrive

Come in off the towpath, and everything arrives at once.

A glass of Forget de Brimont Champagne, then soda bread and brown butter, smoked trout, Coombeshead Farm ham, Montgomery cheddar dumplings with Tunworth custard, and burrata with Todolí citrus. All for sharing.

Stay a while

Roast duck at the table, then one more glass.

Duck with creamed sprouts and quince, potatoes fried in duck fat, brown butter cake to finish. There’s a full vegetarian menu too. Nobody cooks, nobody washes up.
